Sections come from folders. Make a folder inside graphics/ named for the section — graphics/2024/, graphics/tedxuw/ — drop your images in, and push. Sections are listed oldest first by folder name, so years sort themselves.

Titles come from filenames: tedxuw-poster.png shows up as “TEDxUW Poster”. You can edit any title or add a caption afterwards in data/graphics.json and it'll stick.
